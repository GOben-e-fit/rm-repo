# KPI Market Intel

Use this skill when building competitor intelligence, benchmark feeds or market
positioning for KPI Enterprise Mining.

## Sources

- Public competitor pages and docs
- Curated benchmark providers
- Customer-private peer groups
- Crawled public market evidence via Crawl4AI
- Nimble or other approved data feeds

## Rules

- Browse current sources when claims may have changed.
- Keep benchmark provenance explicit; do not invent benchmark values.
- Separate public market evidence from customer-private peer data.
- Store source URL, capture timestamp, extract hash and tenant access policy for evidence.

## Competitive Questions

- What does the competitor automate?
- Is metric governance native or external?
- Does it support tenant-isolated on-prem operation?
- Does it produce evidence-linked actions or only analytics?
- What buyer pain is underserved?
