# KPI Mining Product

Use this skill for product decisions around KPI Enterprise Mining as a C-Level
Performance Operating System rather than a chart dashboard.

## Product Principles

- Optimize for executive decision loops: what happened, why, what to do, owner, evidence.
- Differentiate through sovereign on-prem/DGX operation, multi-agent automation, tenant isolation, audit trail and action orchestration.
- C-Level screens must be dense, operational and evidence-led. Avoid marketing-only landing pages for the product surface.
- Metric governance is a core product capability, not backend plumbing.

## Core Modules

- KPI discovery and candidate review
- Metric store and data contracts
- Driver-tree canvas and OKR lineage
- Anomaly detection and root cause analysis
- C-Level briefings and board packs
- Action orchestration with HITL approvals
- Tenant admin, model policy and audit/evidence explorer

## Roadmap Shape

- v1: restored app, canonical routes, OpenAPI, demo data, smoke tests.
- v2: real sources, data contracts, metric store, reports and lineage.
- v3: autonomous agents, benchmark feeds, action workflows and governance gates.

## Competitive Lens

Compare against ValueWorks, Qlik, DQLabs, BDB and classic BI by asking:

- Does the product move from insight to owned action?
- Is the explanation evidence-linked and replayable?
- Can regulated tenants run sensitive analysis locally?
- Are definitions governed and reconciled before executives use them?
